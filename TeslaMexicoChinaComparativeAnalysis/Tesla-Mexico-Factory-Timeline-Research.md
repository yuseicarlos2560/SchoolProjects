# Tesla’s Planned Mexico Factory: Sourced Event Timeline

## Scope and current status

This timeline concerns the Tesla vehicle factory announced for **Santa Catarina, Nuevo León**, near Monterrey. It covers public events from **October 2022 through November 25, 2026**.

The project was publicly announced in February–March 2023, received environmental and land-use approvals, and was granted state incentives. Tesla later slowed and then paused the project. **The vehicle factory had not been built by the latest status reports in this timeline.** The project was still described as paused, not formally cancelled; in November 2026, Nuevo León’s governor discussed a possible conversion to a data-center project without announcing a defined investment amount. ([El Debate, September 29, 2026](https://www.debate.com.mx/8-motivos-por-los-que-la-planta-de-tesla-en-nuevo-leon-permanece-en-pausa-indefinida-t202609290014.html); [MVS Noticias, November 25, 2026](https://mvsnoticias.com/nuevo-leon/2026/11/25/tesla-en-nuevo-leon-samuel-garcia-espera-reactivar-el-proyecto-en-enero-de-2027-754676.html))

## Event types

- **Geopolitical / trade:** U.S. tariff actions, U.S. election tariff proposals, and the USMCA review.
- **Tesla / Musk:** Musk’s meetings and declarations, Tesla announcements, earnings-call statements, and Tesla filings.
- **Mexican policy / government:** Federal and Nuevo León government statements, permits, incentives, and stated project plans.

## Timeline

### 2022

- **October 22–24, 2022 — Tesla / Musk.** Reuters reported that Musk had met Nuevo León Governor Samuel García and other local officials, as well as U.S. Ambassador Ken Salazar. The reports concerned a possible investment; they did not announce a factory agreement. ([Automotive News](https://www.autonews.com/manufacturing/tesla-factory-mexico-elon-musk-meets-officials/); [Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/news/why-elon-musk-in-monterrey-mexico/))

### 2023

- **February 20, 2023 — Mexican policy / government.** President Andrés Manuel López Obrador said Nuevo León’s water availability was a constraint for a Tesla plant and identified Hidalgo and southeastern Mexico as alternatives. ([Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/english/mexico-to-warn-musk-of-water-shortages-suggest-alternative-sites-for-tesla-plant/); [Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/business/nuevo-leon-not-best-mx-option-tesla/))
- **February 27–28, 2023 — Tesla / Musk.** Musk and López Obrador spoke by telephone before the plant announcement. López Obrador said Musk committed that all water used in vehicle manufacturing would be recycled. ([El Financiero](https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/empresas/2023/02/28/fabrica-de-tesla-en-mexico-los-compromisos-que-musk-hizo-con-amlo/))
- **February 28, 2023 — Mexican policy / government.** López Obrador publicly announced that Tesla would build a plant near Monterrey in Nuevo León and repeated the recycled-water commitment. ([Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/english/tesla-to-build-plant-in-nuevo-leon-state-mexicos-amlo-says/); [The National / AP](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/technology/2023/02/28/tesla-to-build-plant-in-mexico-lopez-obrador-says/))
- **March 1, 2023 — Tesla / Musk.** Musk confirmed at Tesla’s Investor Day that the company’s next Gigafactory would be built in Nuevo León, Mexico. The facility was presented for Tesla’s next-generation vehicle. ([Rho Motion](https://rhomotion.com/news/tesla-investor-day-2023-new-gigafactory-confirmed-among-a-focus-on-innovation-and-efficiency/); [Barron’s Investor Day coverage](https://www.barrons.com/livecoverage/tesla-investor-day))
- **July 31, 2023 — Tesla / Musk.** Tesla Manufacturing Mexico submitted environmental documentation for an industrial building on approximately 260 hectares in Santa Catarina. The filing initiated an authorization process; it was not a construction start. ([Forbes México](https://forbes.com.mx/filial-de-tesla-pide-autorizacion-ambiental-para-su-fabrica-en-nuevo-leon/))
- **September 26, 2023 — Mexican policy / government.** Mexico’s Environment Ministry, SEMARNAT, granted environmental-impact approvals for the planned factory. The approvals included conditions and allowed 26 months to prepare the site and begin construction; other licenses were still required. ([BNamericas](https://www.bnamericas.com/en/news/tesla-obtiene-permisos-ambientales-para-su-gigafabrica-en-mexico))
- **October 18, 2023 — Tesla / Musk.** On Tesla’s Q3 earnings call, Musk said Tesla was laying groundwork but wanted to assess the global economy before going “full tilt” on the Mexico factory. He cited high interest rates and their effect on vehicle affordability. He still said Tesla intended to build the plant. ([Q3 2023 earnings-call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2023/10/18/tesla-tsla-q3-2023-earnings-call-transcript/))
- **December 11–12, 2023 — Mexican policy / government.** SEMARNAT issued land-use permits covering approximately 261 hectares for the planned factory. ([Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/business/land-use-permits-issued-for-tesla-gigafactory-in-nuevo-leon/); [Reuters, December 14](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/mexican-state-grants-tesla-153-224248367.html))
- **December 14, 2023 — Mexican policy / government.** Nuevo León approved **2.627 billion pesos** in incentives, including payroll-tax relief and support for basic infrastructure and roads. Reuters reported that the state estimated Tesla’s investment at more than US$5 billion, while Tesla had not provided a price tag. ([Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/mexican-state-grants-tesla-153-224248367.html))

### 2024

- **2024 U.S. election campaign — Geopolitical / trade.** Donald Trump proposed tariffs on vehicles imported from Mexico. Musk subsequently cited these potential tariffs when explaining Tesla’s pause. This entry records the policy proposal rather than an enacted tariff. ([Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/business/musk-says-tesla-gigafactory-in-mexico-paused-because-of-potential-trump-tariffs/))
- **July 23, 2024 — Tesla / Musk.** Musk said on Tesla’s Q2 earnings call: “We’re currently on pause on Giga Mexico.” He said Tesla needed to see the result of the U.S. election and referred to potential tariffs on vehicles produced in Mexico. ([Q2 2024 earnings-call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2024/07/24/tesla-tsla-q2-2024-earnings-call-transcript/); [Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/business/musk-says-tesla-gigafactory-in-mexico-paused-because-of-potential-trump-tariffs/))

### 2025

- **March 26, 2025; effective April 3, 2025 — Geopolitical / trade.** U.S. Presidential Proclamation 10908 imposed a 25% tariff on imported passenger vehicles and light trucks, effective April 3. For USMCA-qualifying vehicles, the measure provided a process for the tariff to apply only to non-U.S. content. ([White House proclamation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/adjusting-imports-of-automobiles-and-autombile-parts-into-the-united-states/); [White House fact sheet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/03/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-adjusts-imports-of-automobiles-and-automobile-parts-into-the-united-states/))

### 2026

- **January 29, 2026 — Tesla / Musk.** Tesla’s 2025 Form 10-K listed its primary manufacturing facilities as Gigafactory Texas, Fremont Factory, Gigafactory Nevada, Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg, and Gigafactory Shanghai. The table did not list an operating Mexican factory. ([Tesla 2025 Form 10-K, SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm))
- **May 7, 2026 — Mexican policy / government.** Governor Samuel García acknowledged that the Tesla project remained paused and described it as “only paused,” not cancelled. ([MVS Noticias](https://mvsnoticias.com/nuevo-leon/2026/5/7/samuel-garcia-asegura-que-tesla-solo-esta-pausada-en-nuevo-leon-731662.html); [Telediario](https://www.telediario.mx/economia/samuel-garcia-reconoce-proyecto-tesla-en-nl-sigue-en-pausa))
- **July 1, 2026 — Geopolitical / trade.** At the USMCA joint review, the United States did not agree to renew the agreement in its current form. USTR stated that the agreement nevertheless remained in force while the parties addressed the issues. ([USTR](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/july/ambassador-greer-issues-statement-usmca-joint-review))
- **November 25, 2026 — Mexican policy / government.** Governor García said the paused Tesla project could be converted into a data-center project and that the state would seek to reactivate it in January 2027. He said the investment amount had not been defined. This was a statement by the governor, not a Tesla factory announcement. ([MVS Noticias](https://mvsnoticias.com/nuevo-leon/2026/11/25/tesla-en-nuevo-leon-samuel-garcia-espera-reactivar-el-proyecto-en-enero-de-2027-754676.html))

## Distinctions that must be preserved in the slide

- The October 2022 meetings were reported investment discussions, not a signed factory agreement.
- The February 28 announcement was made by Mexico’s president; Tesla’s own Investor Day confirmation followed on March 1.
- Environmental and land-use approvals authorized steps in the permitting process; they did not demonstrate that factory construction had begun.
- Musk’s October 2023 statement delayed going “full tilt” but still affirmed an intention to build. His July 2024 statement explicitly said the project was paused.
- The 2024 tariff entry was a campaign proposal. The 2025 automobile tariff was an enacted measure.
- The November 2026 data-center conversion was a possibility described by Nuevo León’s governor, not a confirmed Tesla vehicle-factory restart.
