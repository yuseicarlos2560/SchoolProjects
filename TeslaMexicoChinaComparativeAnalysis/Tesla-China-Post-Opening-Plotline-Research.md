# Tesla China After Gigafactory Shanghai Opened: Sourced Research Record

## 1. Scope and metric definitions

Gigafactory Shanghai delivered its first China-made Model 3 vehicles on **December 30, 2019**. This research record covers the full calendar years **2020–2025**. ([China Daily](https://subsites.chinadaily.com.cn/lingang/2019-12/19/c_463228_7.htm))

The available sources use three different measures:

- **China domestic retail sales:** vehicles sold in China, excluding Shanghai exports. Tesla does not consistently publish country-level unit sales; annual figures below are CPCA or company figures reported by the cited outlets.
- **Shanghai wholesale shipments:** China-made Model 3/Y vehicles delivered to the Chinese market plus exports. CPCA describes these as wholesale shipments, not registrations or customer deliveries. They are a public proxy for Shanghai factory throughput, **not an audited annual production total**. ([InsideEVs explanation and 2023 data](https://insideevs.com/news/703863/china-tesla-ev-retail-sales-exports-increased-2023/))
- **Tesla global deliveries:** Tesla’s company-wide annual delivery total, published by Tesla Investor Relations.

The calculable percentage from these public sources is:

> **Shanghai wholesale shipments ÷ Tesla global deliveries × 100**

This percentage describes Shanghai’s share of global deliveries using wholesale shipments as the numerator. It must not be labelled as an exact production share.

### 2020 Shanghai numerator

CnEVPost reported that Shanghai delivered **484,130 vehicles in 2021, up 235% year on year**. The implied 2020 base is:

> 484,130 ÷ (1 + 2.35) = **144,516.42**, rounded to **~144,516 vehicles**.

This is an implied value, not a separately published annual total. Any chart using it must mark it as approximate.

### 2021 source discrepancy

Two different 2021 Shanghai totals appear in otherwise attributable reporting:

- **484,130:** company data supplied to CnEVPost; CnEVPost also reports the corresponding **51.7%** share of Tesla’s global deliveries. ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2022/01/12/giga-shanghai-contributes-51-7-of-teslas-2021-deliveries-with-over-480000-vehicles/))
- **473,078:** CPCA figure reported by CnEVPost in a separate article and used as the comparison base for 2022 growth. ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2022/02/07/teslas-revenue-in-china-reaches-13-8-billion-in-2021-up-107-8-year-on-year/))

Using 473,078 ÷ 936,172 gives **50.5%**, not 51.7%. The two totals must not be silently treated as identical.

## 3. Calculated Shanghai wholesale share of Tesla global deliveries

| Year | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| 2020 | ~144,516 ÷ 499,550 | **~28.9%** |
| 2021 | 484,130 ÷ 936,172 | **51.7%** |
| 2022 | 710,865 ÷ 1,313,851 | **54.1%** |
| 2023 | 947,742 ÷ 1,808,581 | **52.4%** |
| 2024 | 916,660 ÷ 1,789,226 | **51.2%** |
| 2025 | 851,732 ÷ 1,636,129 | **52.1%** |

These are calculated values, rounded to one decimal place. The 2021 row uses the company-supplied 484,130 numerator documented above.
