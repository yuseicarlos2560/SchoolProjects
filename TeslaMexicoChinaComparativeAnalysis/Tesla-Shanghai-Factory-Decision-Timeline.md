# Tesla Shanghai Factory: Timeline Preceding the July 2018 Agreement

## Scope and event types

This timeline ends with the agreements signed in Shanghai on **July 10, 2018**. It records documented events without assigning causal weight to them.

- **Geopolitical / trade:** U.S.–China tariff actions and import-tariff changes.
- **China domestic policy:** Chinese rules governing foreign ownership of vehicle manufacturers.
- **Tesla / Musk:** Tesla statements, business disclosures, meetings, talks, registrations, and agreements.

Dates refer to the event itself when the source distinguishes it from the publication date. Plans and exploratory talks are identified as such, not as completed factory agreements.

## Timeline

### 2014

- **April 21, 2014 — Tesla / Musk.** Elon Musk met China’s Minister of Science and Technology, Wan Gang, in Beijing. Wan presented China’s EV-development objectives, policies, and demonstration projects; Musk presented Tesla’s performance and development in China. They discussed EV technology, standards, supporting facilities, and the innovation ecosystem. ([Chinese Academy of Science and Technology for Development](http://www.casted.org.cn/channel/newsinfo/5670))
- **April 21, 2014 — Tesla / Musk.** Speaking in Beijing, Musk said: “At some point in the next three or four years, we’ll be establishing local manufacturing in China.” At the time, China’s tariff on imported vehicles was 25%. ([Bloomberg report republished by the Arkansas Democrat-Gazette](https://www.arkansasonline.com/news/2014/apr/22/tesla-build-cars-china-3-4-years-its-ceo--20140422/))

### 2016

- **June 21, 2016 — Tesla / Musk.** Shanghai Jinqiao Export Processing Zone Development Co. denied a report that its parent, Jinqiao Group, had signed a non-binding factory agreement with Tesla. It stated that Jinqiao Group had signed no agreement of any kind with Tesla. A Tesla spokesman separately confirmed that Tesla was talking to various parties about local production but declined to give details. ([Reuters](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/chinese-firm-denies-agreement-tesla-build-factory-095359882--sector.html))

### 2017

- **April 25, 2017 — Tesla / Musk.** Musk met Chinese Vice Premier Wang Yang in Beijing. The official announcement recorded the meeting but did not announce a factory agreement. ([State Council / Xinhua](https://english.www.gov.cn/state_council/vice_premiers/2017/04/26/content_281475637328724.htm))
- **June 22, 2017 — Tesla / Musk.** Tesla publicly confirmed that it was in talks with the Shanghai municipal government to explore a local automobile factory. Tesla said it expected to have more clearly defined China production plans by the end of 2017 and that most production was expected to remain in the United States. ([Caixin](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2017-06-23/tesla-confirms-talks-with-shanghai-exploring-auto-factory-in-china-101104667.html))
- **November 1, 2017 — Tesla / Musk.** On Tesla’s third-quarter earnings call, Musk described production in China in “about 3 years” as a rough target. He said Tesla expected to increase China investment in 2019, identified Model 3 and potentially Model Y as the planned products, and gave an expected capacity of at least “a couple of hundred thousand vehicles a year, maybe more.” ([Earnings-call report](https://electrek.co/2017/11/02/tesla-factory-china-elon-musk/); [Tesla Q3 2017 update and call notice filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000156459017020822/tsla-ex991_6.htm))
- **Fiscal year 2017 — Tesla / Musk.** Tesla’s 2017 Form 10-K reported **US$2.027 billion in China revenue**, compared with **US$1.065 billion in 2016**. ([Tesla 2017 Form 10-K, SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000156459018002956/tsla-10k_20171231.htm))

### 2018

- **March 8, 2018 — Tesla / Musk.** Musk posted a series of messages to U.S. President Donald Trump about automobile trade. Musk stated that an American car exported to China faced a 25% import duty, while a Chinese car entering the United States faced a 2.5% duty. He also wrote that no U.S. auto company was allowed to own even 50% of its factory in China. ([Fortune, including the text of Musk’s posts](https://fortune.com/2018/03/08/elon-musk-twitter-donald-trump-tariffs-cars-china/))
- **March 22, 2018 — Geopolitical / trade.** President Trump signed a Section 301 memorandum directing U.S. actions in response to Chinese practices concerning technology transfer, intellectual property, and innovation. The actions included proposed additional 25% duties on Chinese products. ([USTR Section 301 fact sheet](https://ustr.gov/about-us/policy-offices/press-office/fact-sheets/2018/march/section-301-fact-sheet))
- **April 4, 2018 — Geopolitical / trade.** China announced a proposed additional 25% tariff on 106 U.S. products under 14 categories, worth approximately US$50 billion and including automobiles. China said implementation would depend on when the United States imposed its proposed tariffs. ([State Council / Xinhua](https://english.www.gov.cn/state_council/ministries/2018/04/04/content_281476101320014.htm))
- **April 17, 2018 — China domestic policy.** The National Development and Reform Commission announced that foreign-shareholding limits for special-purpose vehicle and new-energy vehicle manufacturers would be removed in 2018. It also announced schedules for commercial vehicles in 2020 and other passenger vehicles in 2022. ([State Council / Xinhua](https://english.www.gov.cn/state_council/ministries/2018/04/18/content_281476115987782.htm))
- **May 10, 2018 — Tesla / Musk.** Tesla registered **Tesla (Shanghai) Co. Ltd.** in Shanghai’s free-trade-zone area. The registration listed Tesla Motors HK Limited as sole shareholder, Tesla China head Tom Zhu (Zhu Xiaotong) as legal representative, and activities involving electric cars, spare parts, and batteries. Tesla told Reuters that it had nothing new to add about the registration; Reuters reported that its connection to the anticipated factory was not clear. ([Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/TESLA-INC-6344549/news/Tesla-registers-Shanghai-electric-car-firm-ahead-of-ownership-rule-change-26578625/))
- **May 22, 2018; effective July 1, 2018 — Geopolitical / trade.** China’s Customs Tariff Commission announced a reduction of the general import tariff on complete vehicles from 25% or 20% to **15%**, effective July 1. ([Customs Tariff Commission Announcement No. 3 of 2018](https://www.gov.cn/guowuyuan/2018-05/22/content_5292720.htm))
- **June 15–16, 2018 — Geopolitical / trade.** On June 15, the United States finalized an additional 25% tariff on approximately US$34 billion of Chinese imports, effective July 6. On June 16, China announced additional 25% tariffs on approximately US$50 billion of U.S. goods; the first approximately US$34 billion tranche, including automobiles, was scheduled for July 6. ([USTR, June 15](https://ustr.gov/about-us/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2018/june/ustr-issues-tariffs-chinese-products); [China Customs Tariff Commission Announcement No. 5 of 2018, June 16](http://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2018/art_9ec2d5d22aab417cbe4028ceb5dd416f.html))
- **June 28, 2018; effective July 28, 2018 — China domestic policy.** The NDRC and Ministry of Commerce issued the **Special Administrative Measures (Negative List) for Foreign Investment Access (2018 Edition)**. Item 12 retained the Chinese-shareholding requirement of at least 50% for complete-vehicle manufacturing **except special-purpose vehicles and new-energy vehicles**. The measure was scheduled to take effect on July 28. ([NDRC/MOFCOM Order No. 18 and the official negative list](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2018/content_5317104.htm))
- **July 6, 2018 — Geopolitical / trade.** The United States and China implemented the first US$34 billion tranches of their additional 25% tariffs. China’s measures included U.S.-built automobiles. Combined with the general 15% vehicle tariff, the additional 25% duty produced a **40% import duty on U.S.-built cars**. ([USTR](https://ustr.gov/about-us/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2018/july/ustr-releases-product-exclusion); [AP’s contemporaneous account of the 15% duty and additional 25% measure](https://apnews.com/0ee13b6ad11b42d5b7dd74634a180156))
- **July 10, 2018 — Tesla / Musk.** Tesla and Shanghai authorities signed agreements for Tesla to establish an independently operated plant integrating research and development, manufacturing, and sales. The announced planned annual capacity was **500,000 vehicles**. Musk attended the signing at Shanghai’s Fairmont Peace Hotel; Shanghai Mayor Ying Yong and other Chinese officials also attended. AP reported that the agreement made Tesla the first wholly foreign-owned automaker in China. Tesla also announced a Shanghai research-and-development facility. No financial details were announced. ([Reuters](https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/companies/2018-07-10-elon-musk-heads-to-china-the-biggest-market-for-teslas-electric-vehicles/); [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/0ee13b6ad11b42d5b7dd74634a180156))
